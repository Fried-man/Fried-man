Hey! I'm **Andrew Friedman**, a Senior Software Engineer at [GEICO](https://github.com/Geico) and a Computer Science graduate of the Georgia Institute of Technology where I studied Computer Architecture and Artificial Intelligence. At work, I spend most of my time architecting GEICO's next-generation cross-platform Flutter platform. At home, I build stuff for fun (usually with [Flutter](https://flutter.dev))!

I began programming recreationally in 2012 and since then my love for computing has only grown stronger. Over the last decade I've shipped 100+ apps to the public, contributed to large-scale enterprise monorepos used by 100+ engineers, and made some contributions to open source!

Please take some time to explore my profile and get a sense of the work I'm passionate about. Thank you for stopping by and I hope you have a wonderful day!
